Our practice areas

Select an area to see how we can help.

Contracts

Drafting, review, negotiation and enforcement

We draft, review and negotiate contracts that protect your interests and leave no room for doubt. When an agreement is breached, we move quickly on enforcement and dispute resolution. We also prepare tailored contracts for businesses across a range of industries.

Company law

Formation, governance, acquisitions and disputes

We guide businesses through formation, corporate governance, acquisitions, shareholder disputes and dissolution, so your company operates within the law, your personal assets stay protected and you can focus on growth.

Wills and deceased estates

Wills, trusts and estate administration

We draft wills, establish trusts and administer deceased estates with meticulous attention to detail. Working closely with you, we build a plan that protects your assets, provides for your loved ones and ensures your wishes are carried out exactly.

Family law

Divorce, custody, maintenance and adoption

Family matters are personal and often emotionally charged. We handle divorce proceedings, child custody arrangements, spousal maintenance and adoption with the sensitivity they demand, guiding you through difficult times with compassion and clarity.

Criminal law

Defence from first consultation to appeal

We defend individuals facing charges ranging from minor offences to serious crimes. Our services cover:

  • Pre-trial consultations and bail applications
  • Courtroom advocacy
  • Plea negotiations
  • Appeals

We understand what is at stake and work to build a robust defence that protects your rights and secures a fair trial.

Eviction law

For landlords and tenants

We represent both landlords and tenants through the eviction process, from drafting and enforcing notices to handling disputes and ensuring every legal requirement is met. Whether you need to lawfully regain possession of your property or defend against an unjust eviction, we provide strategic counsel and steady representation.

Pension fund law

Claims, complaints and benefit disputes

We assist members and beneficiaries with pension and provident fund matters, including withheld or delayed benefits, death benefit distributions and complaints against funds, so you receive what you are entitled to.

Litigation and dispute resolution

Civil disputes, in and out of court

When disputes arise, you need a skilled attorney in your corner. We handle a wide range of civil litigation, from contract disputes to personal injury claims, and pursue settlement where it serves you best, working tirelessly for the strongest possible outcome.

Founder and Director

Andile Vilakazi

Andile founded Binda Attorneys in 2023, establishing the firm's main offices in Newcastle. He was admitted as an attorney in 2021 after completing his LLB at the University of Pretoria.

His experience spans contract law, litigation, company law, family law, wills and estates, land reform, eviction, criminal law and pension funds, giving clients one trusted adviser across the matters that shape their lives and businesses.
